Keep track of spending, income, money lent and money borrowed. Start on your phone. Connect your own Google Drive only if you choose.

A clearer money picture

Month-by-month income and spending, daily entries, charts, budgets and goals. Keep repayments linked to the right person and debt.

A ledger that feels like yours

Choose colours, fonts, appearance and categories. Export a printable PDF or CSV. Save a passphrase-encrypted backup with your customization.

Local first. Drive optional.

No AiCoN account server, ads or analytics. Optional sync uses Hisab's private app-data folder in your own Google Drive, not access to all your files. Connecting an account does not automatically copy your separate local ledger.

Reminders stay in your hands

Record a pay-by date and ask your phone to remind you. SMS and WhatsApp reminders open for review. You tap Send in your messaging app. The 0.2.2-dev build has no automatic SMS sender or SMS permission.

Keep a safe copy

Encrypted backup files can be saved wherever Android's file picker offers a supported location. This is manual backup and restore, not automatic sync with every cloud provider. CSV and PDF are readable exports, not restore backups. AiCoN cannot recover a lost backup passphrase.

What Hisab does not do

Hisab does not connect to your bank, read SMS or notifications, automatically capture payments, or send messages in the background. Drive sync is for your own account, not a shared ledger between people. Figures depend on the entries you record. Hisab is not financial advice.
